## My favorite series is "My Special Tatay" by Agnes G. Uligan

This series is about a father with special needs *(mild intellectual disability)* that suddenly learns that he has a child to take care of. It stars Ken Chan playing as "Boyet" the father of the child, and follows the story of how a man with different capabilities than others take care of a child with the help of the people around him. The series also tackles many themes that are relevant to people with special needs. It spanned the length of 150 episodes, ending with "Boyet" not fulfilling his dreams of becoming a pilot, but realizing his family's dreams through Angelo, their son.

### This series is *special* to me because:
- It has one of if not the best lines in Philippine television ==Di ka na pokpok aubrey eh==.
- It shows how even people with disabilities still have emotional intelligence . :heartpulse:
- It shows how different life is with disabilities. :cry:
